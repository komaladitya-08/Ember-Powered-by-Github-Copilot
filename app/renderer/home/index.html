Before Ember starts

  • It reads the window you're working in through the desktop's accessibility service, and for tasks it may take a screenshot of it. Windows on your never-look list and password prompts are never read.
  • When you ask something, your request and what Ember read from the screen go to GitHub Copilot to work out the answer. Nothing is sent before you ask.
  • It asks before acting in an app, and always before anything hard to undo. It never types passwords. Ctrl+Alt+X stops everything at once.
  • On this computer it keeps your settings, logs and a record of every action it took (kept 30 days) in ~/.config/ember. What you ask and what Ember answers is kept only while it runs. It sends no usage statistics.
  • The microphone stays off unless you turn on “Hey Ember” below.
  • Other computers and commands are off until you turn them on: Ember can work in WSL, on computers you reach over SSH, and run commands in its own window. Each asks you first.

Conversation

    Kept only while Ember runs: closing Ember forgets it. Signing out forgets it too.

    Voice

    Ember will keep the microphone open while it runs, listening only for “Hey Ember” and “Ember, stop”. That listening happens on this computer: no audio leaves it, and nothing is recorded. After you say “Hey Ember”, what you say next is turned into text on this computer, and only that text is sent to Copilot. After Ember answers, you have 8 seconds to follow up without saying “Hey Ember” again. To tell its own voice and your speakers apart from you, Ember also listens to what your speakers play (on this computer only). GNOME shows a microphone icon while Ember listens. Ctrl+Alt+M turns the microphone off and on.

    Off

    Say “Hey Ember, …” and your request, or “Ember, stop” to stop everything.

        Computers

        Ember can work on this computer, in WSL, and on computers you reach over SSH. Your voice and your GitHub sign-in always stay here.

          Commands

          Ember runs commands in its own command window, where you can watch and stop them. It asks first before anything that deletes, publishes, touches secrets or changes the system.

          Motion and keys

          When Ember asks “May I?”, press Ctrl+Alt+Y to allow or Ctrl+Alt+N for not now; you don't need the mouse. With a screen reader running, Ember also speaks its questions and results.
