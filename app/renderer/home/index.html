Before Ember starts

  • It reads the window you're working in through the desktop's accessibility service, and for tasks it may take a screenshot of it. Windows on your never-look list and password prompts are never read.
  • When you ask something, your request and what Ember read from the screen go to GitHub Copilot to work out the answer. Nothing is sent before you ask.
  • It asks before acting in an app, and always before anything hard to undo. It never types passwords. Ctrl+Alt+X stops everything at once.
  • On this computer it keeps your settings, logs and a record of every action it took (kept 30 days) in ~/.config/ember. It sends no usage statistics.
  • The microphone stays off unless you turn on “Hey Ember” below.

Your Ember

Press Ctrl+Alt+E any time to come back here, or click Ember's handle in the bottom-right corner.

Copilot

Checking…

Ask Ember anything with Ctrl+Alt+Space.

Voice

Ember will keep the microphone open while it runs, listening only for “Hey Ember” and “Ember, stop”. That listening happens on this computer: no audio leaves it, and nothing is recorded. After you say “Hey Ember”, what you say next is turned into text on this computer, and only that text is sent to Copilot. GNOME shows a microphone icon while Ember listens. Ctrl+Alt+M turns the microphone off and on.

Off

Say “Hey Ember, …” and your request, or “Ember, stop” to stop everything.

What Ember did

    Every decision and action is also in ~/.config/ember/audit/ and in the system journal (journalctl -t ember-audit).
