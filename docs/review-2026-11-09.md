# Product audit: seventh round

9 November 2026. Starting main: `36451257` (PR #204). Command safety baseline: `4d64d3a`.

finding inventory and walked first-use, everyday and failure journeys. Source inspection, executable classifier/service
probes, Chromium renders, actual Electron/mock-provider journeys and synthetic speech tests are distinguished from
native-device validation. Findings were verified before repair; each repair has a named regression observed failing.

records the changes and remaining limits. The [starting findings and dispositions](review-2026-11-09-findings.md)
preserve each area's file/line evidence, verification method, challenge corrections and path to 9.

A 9 means no P1 or P2 remains in the assessed area. P3 polish, native-only acceptance and the recorded owner decisions
do not cap a score. A finite command corpus is evidence over its cases, not a proof over arbitrary shell programs.
